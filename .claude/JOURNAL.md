14. **Task [Extended] - Memory at open on a page with a large script** (v1.0.17): opening a 13 MB page holding a 10.5 MB script took over 1.3 GB of browser heap; parse5 now takes runs of plain characters in one piece, and the carried reading of entry 13 is removed<br>

After: 122 MB, page shown after 0.48 s. A full reading of an 8.9 MB page then costs about 25 ms, and a build without `carryAnalysis` saved comments as fast as one with it, so `carryAnalysis`, `editsOf`, `IRun.splittable`, `IAnalysis.insertions` and the stripped-text memo were removed and ACC-STORE-74 rejected as superseded; the edits route, `applyEdits`, `markerEdits` and DEF-STORE-12 stay.
